The secondary twin of [`../../primary-sources/china-official-records-1933-1987/SOURCE.md`](../../primary-sources/china-official-records-1933-1987/SOURCE.md): the narration of
reporters and editors, the later captions and the histories in its items. The files of the source stay
in the primary folder.

- **Status:** made by the reading of the source search of 2026-11. A checker checked a sample of the
  facts of each chunk.

## What it rests on

Each item says what its writer used, where the item tells it.

## Limits

The writers of these parts wrote later than the events, or did not see them.
