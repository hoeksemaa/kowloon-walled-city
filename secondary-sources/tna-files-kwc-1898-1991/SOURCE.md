### gwulo-11: Proposed public park in Kowloon: question of Chinese jurisdiction in city area (CO 129/544/14), in "The National Archives - Historic Hong Kong documents"

- **Status:** Read on 2026-11-20; a checker checked a sample of the facts on 2026-11-20.
- **Citation:** David (Gwulo), 'The National Archives - Historic Hong Kong documents', section 'Arguing with China about Kowloon City', Gwulo, 11 Oct 2021, https://gwulo.com/node/56213 ; on The National Archives, CO 129/544/14, 'Proposed public park in Kowloon: question of Chinese jurisdiction in city area', 1933 Aug. 9-Sept., https://discovery.nationalarchives.gov.uk/details/r/C2329775 (read 2026-11-20).
- **Class:** secondary (A 2021 summary of the 1933 file by a visitor to Kew. It quotes two primary texts (the convention of 1898 and a telegram of 1899). The file (primary) is the reason to keep the item in tna-files-kwc-1898-1991; the article can also go to secondary-sources/.)
- **Date of the record:** file of 1933 (TNA: 1933 Aug. 9-Sept.); article of 11 Oct 2021
- **Note:** The secondary part of the item of the same id in [`../../primary-sources/tna-files-kwc-1898-1991/SOURCE.md`](../../primary-sources/tna-files-kwc-1898-1991/SOURCE.md).

| section 'Arguing with China about Kowloon City' | The Hong Kong Government announced that the residents of Kowloon City would be evicted to make room for a public park. | "residents of Kowloon City were going to be evicted to make space for a new public park" |
| section 'Arguing with China about Kowloon City' | The residents went to the Chinese government. | section 'Arguing with China about Kowloon City' | The British officials did not agree among themselves. One held that a telegram of 30 May 1899 to the Chinese side overruled the clause. | "another believed it was overruled by a telegram sent to the Chinese side on 30 May 1899" |
| section 'Arguing with China about Kowloon City', quotation of the 1899 telegram | The telegram of 30 May 1899 said that Britain could not allow Chinese authority inside the walls again, because the Chinese garrison had given no protection. | "it is impossible for His Majesty's Government to allow the resumption of Chinese authority within the Walls of that city." |
| section 'Arguing with China about Kowloon City' | The file records no final decision. The author knows of no park built there in the 1930s and guesses that the plan was shelved. | "Frustratingly, the file doesn't note any final decision." |
| TNA Discovery record C2329775 | The TNA catalogue dates the file 9 August to September 1933. Its former department reference is 13832, and it is not digitised. | "1933 Aug. 9-Sept." |
