- **Status:** Read on 2026-11-20; a checker checked a sample of the facts on 2026-11-20.
- **Citation:** Anne Bourgeois-Vignon (text), Patrick Zachmann (photos), 'So Long, China', Magnum Photos, Arts & Culture, 28 October 2016; gallery of 23 photos, images par45982 ('The Walled City', 1987) and par47473 (Ah Sai, 1987); https://www.magnumphotos.com/arts-culture/society-arts-culture/patrick-zachmann-so-long-china/

- **Date of the record:** 1987 (photos, as captioned); page published 2016-10-28
