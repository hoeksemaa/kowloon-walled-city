### ja-1: 香港九龍城スラムの空間構成 (Spatial Structure of the Kowloon Walled City Slum, Hong Kong)

- **Status:** Read on 2026-11-20; a checker checked a sample of the facts on 2026-11-20.
- **Citation:** Shunichi J. Watanabe (渡辺俊一) and Masahiro Ibayashi (居林昌宏), 「香港九龍城スラムの空間構成」 (Spatial Structure of the Kowloon Walled City Slum, Hong Kong), 『都市計画論文集』 (City Planning Institute of Japan) no. 28, 1993, pp.439-444, DOI 10.11361/journalcpij.28.439, https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/journalcpij/28/0/28_439/_pdf/-char/ja
- **Class:** primary (A measured field survey of the empty City made in July-August 1992 by the authors' team, published in 1993: primary for the buildings, lanes, stairs and corridors (but made after the residents left, so not for daily life). The history section and the quoted Government figures are secondary.)
- **Date of the record:** survey 22 Jul - 7 Aug 1992; published 1993 (vol. 28; issue date 1993-10-25 per blocked-b-1)
- **Note:** The secondary part of the item of the same id in [`../../primary-sources/surveys-and-studies-1960-1995/SOURCE.md`](../../primary-sources/surveys-and-studies-1960-1995/SOURCE.md).
