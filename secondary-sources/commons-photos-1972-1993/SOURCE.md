# Wikimedia Commons photographs, 1972–1993: later captions and place notes

This file holds the secondary parts of the Commons pages of the 11 photos of Kowloon Walled
City (九龍城寨) in its twin, [`../../primary-sources/commons-photos-1972-1993/SOURCE.md`](../../primary-sources/commons-photos-1972-1993/SOURCE.md).
The photos are primary. Later writers added words to the pages. The repo does not count these
words as the words of a witness (see "What it rests on"). Here are these words: the captions
of the files credited to Ian Lambot, the image notes, the place categories, a German text and
the structured-data captions. The twin keeps the photos, the file data, the archive data in two
files and the readings of the signs. It also keeps the captions that the photographers Jidanni
and Roger Price wrote about their own photos.

- **Citation:** Wikimedia Commons, "Category:Historical images of Kowloon Walled City", the
  file description pages of the 11 files in the twin folder.
- **Link:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Historical_images_of_Kowloon_Walled_City
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-29, through the Commons API (`prop=imageinfo|revisions`). I read the
  live pages of the 1973, 1975 and 1989 files, and of the Jidanni file, again on 2026-11-16.
  Their captions and notes were the same as in the API answers.
- **Copyright:** the text of Commons pages is CC BY-SA 4.0. Each photo has the license that the
  twin gives.
- **Where the files are:** in the primary folder only,
  `../../primary-sources/commons-photos-1972-1993/`. Each `<name>.commons.json` there is the
  full API answer, with the wikitext of the page. This folder has no files.

## What it rests on

- The user ShockD made the pages of the 7 files credited to Lambot on 14 Feb 2017, with the
  UploadWizard. The captions cite his book *City of Darkness: Life in Kowloon Walled City*
  (1993, ISBN 1-873200-13-7), pages of his website cityofdarkness.co.uk, or both.
- I cannot tell if ShockD wrote the captions or copied them from the website. The website pages
  were 404 on 2026-11-16. My fetch tool cannot read the Wayback Machine. The repo classes the
  captions of Lambot and Girard in the book as secondary (see
  `../../primary-sources/city-of-darkness-revisited/SOURCE.md`). Thus the captions are
  secondary in both cases.
- Lambot first visited the City in the early 1980s (twin of the book, pp270–283). Thus he was
  not a witness of the City of 1972, 1973 and 1975. The data in two of these files names a
  press archive as the source (see the twin, "Notes on reliability").
- The 1975 caption and a caption in the 2014 edition of the book share the words "maximum
  height". The p334 caption (for the 1973 aerial view on p335) gives buildings "rising to the
  maximum height of 12, 13 and 14 storeys" (twin of the book, table "Captions and essays").
- Commons editors added the image notes and the place categories from their own knowledge of
  Hong Kong (香港). They cite no source.
- The Jidanni page has a German description by a later editor. Jidanni's upload of 2006 had
  only the English text. The German text gives no source.

## Limits

- I do not know who wrote each caption, or when the text last changed. The upload dates are
  the only dates.
- The dates in the captions are weak. Commons dates the Alley, Night and Playground files to
  "1993", the year of the book (see the twin). The 1975 caption gives "1975", and the path of
  its web page has "1975" ("government-interventions/1975-02-2"). The SCMP data in the file
  gives only "1970s".
- Two files are scans from press archives: the Sing Tao Daily (星島日報) and the SCMP. Their
  catalogue data is primary, thus it is in the twin ("Notes on reliability"). It limits the
  later captions:
  - The Sing Tao Daily printed the 1972 photo on 25 Dec 1972, page A18. Its caption starts
    "十年前" ("ten years ago"). If this gives the age of the photo, it shows the City of about
    1962.
  - For the 1975 photo, the SCMP gives only "1970s" and a "Post Staff Photographer".
  - Thus the credit "Ian Lambot" on these two pages names the book, not the photographer.
- The 2014 edition of the book credits its 1973 aerial view (p335) and 1975 photo (p336) to the
  Government Information Services (政府新聞處). See the twin of the book. On 2026-11-16 I compared the page scans with the Commons files. They are other photos: p335 looks
  almost straight down, and p336 shows one tower under bamboo from the street.
- No caption gives a source for the name Sai Tau Tsuen. Other parts of the repo agree that Sai
  Tau Tsuen lay outside the City: `tools/context_input.json` has "Sai Tau Tsuen ground
  (cleared)", and `docs/utilities.md` names "Sai Tau Tsuen (西頭村)" (book, p108).
- In the 1989 photo the note "Tung Tau Estate" marks tall new blocks. `tools/context_input.json`
  leaves out "the 1988-89 Tung Tau blocks". Thus these blocks can be later than December 1986.
- The categories of the Jidanni file have no boxes. I cannot tell which hill is which.

## Leads to primary sources

- The Sing Tao Daily (星島日報), 25 Dec 1972, page A18: the printed photo and its caption. The
  Sing Tao archive number is 20798652. The repo does not hold it.
- The SCMP picture library, MPID8221639 ("Post Staff Photographer"): the print and its true
  date. The repo does not hold it.
- *City of Darkness* (1993), ISBN 1-873200-13-7: the credits and captions of these photos. The
  repo holds only the 2014 edition (`../../primary-sources/city-of-darkness-revisited/`).
- The pages of cityofdarkness.co.uk that the captions cite (all 404 on 2026-11-16):
  `/government-interventions/1975-02-2/`, `/order-print/01-aerial-view/`,
  `/dark-alleys/dark-city-04-2/` and `/order-print/art-prints-p02-2/`. An archived copy can
  give the dates of the shots.

## Check (2026-11-16)

A script (`research/sources-2026-11/tmp/check_split_commons_photos.py`) found no lost fact of
the old primary file, and the moved captions and notes are secondary. The archive data of the
1972 and 1975 files is catalogue data, thus I moved it to the twin, with the Commons file
histories. From the JSON, the live pages and the files, I fixed the upload dates, the Sing Tao
caption and the sources of Chinese names. I compared the book scans of p335
and p336 with the Commons files: they are other photos.
