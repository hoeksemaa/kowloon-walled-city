# Press reports of 1959 and 1992: the reporters' history of the City

This file holds the secondary parts of two press reports on Kowloon Walled City (九龍城寨):
TIME (1959) and *The Baltimore Sun* (1992). Its twin is
[`../../primary-sources/press-articles-1959-1992/SOURCE.md`](../../primary-sources/press-articles-1959-1992/SOURCE.md).
The twin keeps what each reporter saw or heard at the time. For 1959, these are the alleys,
the trades and the police raids. For 1992, these are the holdouts, the residents' words and
the figures. Each reporter also told the history of the City, from 1843 to 1987, and its
reputation. The reporters did not see these events, thus these background lines are here.

## Why this is secondary

Each report is primary for what the reporter saw at the time. But the history lines tell of
events before the report. These are the fort of 1843, the lease of 1898 and the British troops
of 1899. They are also the clearance attempts of 1947–48 and 1962, and the 1987 decision. The
reporters were not witnesses of these events. The reputation lines ("Even Hong Kong's police
wouldn't go in", "Few outsiders ventured into the vermin-ridden honeycomb") are general claims
about past decades, with no witness and no date.

## What it rests on

- The two reports cite no source for their history.
- INFERRED: the reporters took the history from earlier press reports and from official
  briefings. The reports do not say this.
- The *Sun* names "Britain's 1898 treaty with China", because the holdouts invoked it.
- TIME names "the original lease".

## Limits

- The year of the first clearance attempt and the fire at the consulate: TIME gives "In 1947",
  and the *Sun* gives "in 1948". The repo holds the *China Mail* front page of 13 Jan 1948,
  "Kowloon Hut City Incident" (CoDR p330, in
  [`../../primary-sources/city-of-darkness-revisited/`](../../primary-sources/city-of-darkness-revisited/SOURCE.md)).
  The primary record wins. Check the year against it.
- The *Sun* gives "1962" for the second attempt. The repo holds two pages of the *Hong Kong
  Standard* of January 1963 (CoDR pp130 and 334). Check the year against them.
- The *Sun* says "founded in 1843 as a Chinese fort". It gives no source. The repo holds a
  survey map of 1845 with the stone fort (CoDR p64) and a plan of 1847 (CoDR p62, a modern
  drawing).
- The two reports disagree on the lease. TIME says that the City "sits in a part of the
  so-called New Territories". The *Sun* says that the leased area "surrounded but did not
  include the Walled City". The text of the 1898 Convention can settle this (see the leads).
- "Even Hong Kong's police wouldn't go in" disagrees with the primary TIME report of 1959 in
  the twin. In August 1959 the police arrested 150 people in one night and began nightly
  patrols. The primary report wins. The *Sun* gives no date for its claim.
- "Gone are the brothels, the opium dens ..." gives no date and no source for each trade. Do
  not use it as a list of the trades of 1987.
- "Mao Tse-tung's wrath", "Beijing's quiet assent" and "China apparently also came to view it
  with embarrassment" are the reporter's reading of events. He names no record.
- TIME uses "Kowloon City" for the Walled City, not for the district of Kowloon City (九龍城)
  around it.

## Leads to primary sources

The reports cite no record. They name one treaty, and the repo can check some points.

- The Convention for the Extension of Hong Kong Territory (展拓香港界址專條), 9 Jun 1898. The
  *Sun* says "That treaty disallowed expulsion of the city's inhabitants". TIME says that "the
  original lease provided for Chinese administration". The repo does not hold the text. Get it.
- The British taking of the fort in 1899. The *Sun* names no record. Look in the UK National
  Archives, for example the Colonial Office series CO 129 (Hong Kong).
- The clearance of 1947–48 and the fire at the British consulate in Canton (廣州). The repo
  holds the *China Mail* page of 13 Jan 1948 (CoDR p330). The government files of 1947–48 are
  not in the repo.
- The attempt of 1962. The repo holds the *Hong Kong Standard* pages of 1963 (CoDR pp130 and
  334). Search the GRS (政府檔案處) catalogue in
  [`../../primary-sources/grs-catalogue-kwc/`](../../primary-sources/grs-catalogue-kwc/SOURCE.md)
  for files of 1962–63.
- The decision of 1987 to clear the City. The repo holds the LegCo (立法局) Hansard of 1987 in
  [`../../primary-sources/legco-hansard-1986-1993/`](../../primary-sources/legco-hansard-1986-1993/SOURCE.md).
  For "Beijing's quiet assent", see FCO 40/2253, "Future of Hong Kong: Kowloon Walled City"
  (1987), in the blocked table of `../../primary-sources/README.md`.
