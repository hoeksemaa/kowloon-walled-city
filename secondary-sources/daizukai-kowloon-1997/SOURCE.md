# 『大図解 九龍城』 (Iwanami Shoten (岩波書店), 1997): the panorama, a reconstruction

This file holds the secondary part of 『大図解 九龍城』. That part is the large cutaway panorama of
Kowloon Walled City (九龍城寨) by Terasawa Hitomi (寺澤一美), with its labels. The file also holds
the English notes of the rioleo.org page that shows it. The panorama is a reconstruction. It
shows the life of the City as the team and the artist believed it was before the clearance. Its
twin,
[`../../primary-sources/daizukai-kowloon-1997/SOURCE.md`](../../primary-sources/daizukai-kowloon-1997/SOURCE.md),
holds the primary part: the survey of the empty City by the 九龍城探検隊 in early 1993.

- **Citation:** 九龍城探検隊 (text), Kani Hiroaki (可児弘明) (supervisor, 監修),
  Terasawa Hitomi (寺澤一美) (drawings), 『大図解 九龍城』. Iwanami Shoten (岩波書店),
  10 Jul 1997. ISBN 9784000080705.
- **Link:** https://www.rioleo.org/kowloon/ ("The Kowloon Walled City panorama (with English
  annotations)"). The page has no date and no author name. It shows the panorama as
  `crosssection.jpg` (4716 × 1754 px). The English notes are in a script of the page, as boxes
  on the image. The book: https://www.kinokuniya.co.jp/f/dsg-01-9784000080705
- **Retrieved:** 2026-11-05: an agent read 24 room labels in `crosssection.jpg` at full size.
  2026-11-16: I read all the labels of the image again, at full size and at 2× zoom. I also read
  the English notes in the script of the page.
- **Copyright:** © the artist and Iwanami Shoten. The rioleo.org page gives no license. It says

  book Daizukan Kyuryujyou".

  `../../primary-sources/daizukai-kowloon-1997/`. That folder holds only its `SOURCE.md`: nobody
  saved the image. This folder holds no files.

## What it rests on

- The intro text of the panorama tells how the artist made it. The text is at the top left of
  `crosssection.jpg` (x 0–600, y 235–390). The left edge of the copy cuts the first character of
  lines 1 and 3 (…).
  - "…実測調査によって、まず東西方向の断面図ができあがった。" (The measured survey first gave
    a cross-section from east to west.)
  - "九龍城が一番生き生きとしていた時の生活を描き込んでみたのがこのパノラマである。" (This
    panorama draws in the life of the City at the time when it was most lively.)
  - "…活の名残をもとに、文献資料や聞き取りの成果を加えてある。" (It rests on the remains of
    [daily] life, with the results of documents and interviews added.)
  - "「なんでもあり」の濃密な都市を形成していたことが分かるだろう。" (You will see that it made
    a dense city where "anything goes".)
- Thus the panorama has three bases: the survey, the things left in the empty rooms, and
  documents and interviews. The panorama pages do not name the documents or the people.
- The English note of rioleo.org on this text: "includes referenced material as well as
  anecdotal content".
- The survey: the 九龍城探検隊, led by Takayuki Suzuki (鈴木隆行), measured a slice of the
  empty City. *City of Darkness Revisited* dates it to January–February 1993 (pp204 and 209).
  The survey is primary. See the twin and `../../primary-sources/city-of-darkness-revisited/` (M89–M93).

## Limits

- **The panorama: a reconstruction.** The artist filled the rooms with people and trades, as
  the City was before the clearance. The book does not say which label comes from the survey
  and which comes from stories. Some labels tell a story, for example a triad office with strip
  joints on the floors above and below (Z46). Thus every label has the basis "guess". Use the
  labels for the kinds of rooms and their mix, not for the address of one room.
- The same artist drew the fold-out of *City of Darkness Revisited* (© 2014 Hitomi Terasawa).
  That book says its fold-out "is far from accurate".
- The panorama gives no year. It shows the City at the time when it was most lively
  (一番生き生きとしていた時).
- Many labels are the words of drawn people, or jokes (「」 in the table below). They give the
  mood of the artist's City. They are not records.
- The copy on rioleo.org has panorama pages 5–6 and 11–12 only. The page numbers 6 and 11 are at
  the foot of the image, at about x 3443 and x 3497. They show that the stitch joins page 6 to
  page 11 at about x 3470. Pages 7–10 are not in the copy. Thus a distance in x across that join
  is wrong.
- The left edge of the copy cuts the intro text. The right edge cuts one label (x 4705, y 40):
  I cannot read it.
- The labels give no house numbers. Japanese floor names count G/F as 1階, thus 2階 is 1/F in
  Hong Kong (香港) usage.
- The English notes of rioleo.org come from an unnamed translator. They leave out the noodle
  factory (Z41), the spinning mill (Z47), the sheet-metal workshop (Z48), the gambling room (Z55)
  and the labels marked * below. They also have these errors:
  - Z61: 入れ歯 are dentures. The note says "The dentist's show-window has a row of fillings on
    display."
  - 祭壇に信仰する神仏を… is a home altar. The note says "Buddhist temples."
  - 屋上にできた中庭 is a courtyard on a roof. The note says "A rooftop garden."
  - Z46: 黒社会 are the triads. The note says "an office for Hong Kong's black market".
  - 鳥籠のように (like birdcages) is missing from the note "A balcony with a roof."
  - The Japanese says that the dark rooms themselves are bright with fluorescent lamps. The note
    says "There are a lot of rooms without any access to outside light, but others are
    illuminated by fluorescent bulbs."
  - Street names: "'Dragon City Road'" for Lung Shing Road (龍城路), and "'Kwon Ming Street'" for
    Kwong Ming Street (光明街).

## The page text of rioleo.org (not the book)

The unnamed author of rioleo.org gives a short history of the City. These facts do not come
from the book, and the page names no source for them.

- "while it stood it was famous for unlicenced practice of medicine, prostitution and the rule
  of gangs"
- "with postal service and police rounds as well as a rudimentary sewage system"
- "In 1987 there were apprently 33,000 inhabitants within its 6.5-acre confines" (sic)
- "or about 120 times the density of New York City today"
- "it was demolished between 1993 and 1994"
