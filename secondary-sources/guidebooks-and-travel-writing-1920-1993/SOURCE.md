- **Status:** Read on 2026-11-20; a checker checked a sample of the facts on 2026-11-20.
- **Citation:** Madge Seaver, 'Kowloon Walled City', Friends Bulletin (Pacific Yearly Meeting of the Religious Society of Friends), Vol. 39, Nos. 7 and 8, April-May 1971, pp. 9-10. Internet Archive item friendsbulletin3978unse_2, https://archive.org/details/friendsbulletin3978unse_2
