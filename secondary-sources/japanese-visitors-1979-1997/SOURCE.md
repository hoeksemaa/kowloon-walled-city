### ja-2: 消えた九龍城砦、そして返還

- **Status:** Read on 2026-11-20; a checker checked a sample of the facts on 2026-11-20.
- **Citation:** 阿佐部伸一 (Asabe Shin'ichi), 「消えた九龍城砦、そして返還 1989 - 1997年」, 『阿佐部伸一　リポート集　東南アジアの人びと』 (asabe.jp), section 「台湾、香港、イギリス、日本」; text and photos by the author; written 1997, web page ©2022; https://asabe.jp/asiareport/%E6%B6%88%E3%81%88%E3%81%9F%E4%B9%9D%E9%BE%8D%E5%9F%8E%E7%A0%A6%E3%80%81%E3%81%9D%E3%81%97%E3%81%A6%E8%BF%94%E9%82%84/
- **Class:** retrospective (A Japanese reporter's first-hand account of his visits and interviews in the City (about 1989-1991, the Yu Lan festival of 11 Aug 1991, the demolition ceremony of 23 Mar 1993), written up in 1997 ('当時' before each age): retrospective (1997). The residents' quoted words were recorded at the time of each interview (kind primary). The history of the fort and the figures from Government counts are secondary. The text gives two dates for the first meeting with 陳美珊: '八十九年' (1989) and 'two years' before March 1993 (1991).)
- **Date of the record:** visits c.1989-1993 (interviews mostly c.1989-1991, inferred); text written 1997; web page ©2022
