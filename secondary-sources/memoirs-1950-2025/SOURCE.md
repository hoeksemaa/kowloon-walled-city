- **Status:** Read on 2026-11-22; a checker checked a sample of the facts on 2026-11-22.
- **Citation:** Gordon Jones, 'The Kowloon City District and the Clearance of the Kowloon Walled City: Personal Recollections', Journal of the Royal Asiatic Society Hong Kong Branch, Vol. 51 (2011), pp. 257-278; HKU Digital Repository, https://digitalrepository.lib.hku.hk/catalog/s752cj54k (canvases 258-279); also https://www.jstor.org/stable/23891943

- **Date of the record:** 2011 (published); recollections of November 1983 - May 1986 and of 14 January 1987
