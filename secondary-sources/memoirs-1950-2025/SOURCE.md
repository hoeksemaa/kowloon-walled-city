### en-testimony-7: The '633' Squad (in Stories from the Royal Hong Kong Police: Fifty accounts from officers of Hong Kong's colonial-era police force)

- **Status:** Read on 2026-11-20; a checker checked a sample of the facts on 2026-11-20.
- **Citation:** John Murray. "The '633' Squad". In: Stories from the Royal Hong Kong Police: Fifty accounts from officers of Hong Kong's colonial-era police force. Hong Kong: Blacksmith Books (with The Royal Hong Kong Police Association), 2020, pp. 156-162. ISBN 978-988-79638-8-2. Free preview PDF.
- **Class:** retrospective (2020) (A former RHKP inspector tells, in 2020, of a raid in the City in November 1973 that he took part in. The history paragraphs are his background, not testimony.)
- **Date of the record:** 2020
