# M+ CA17: Suenn Ho's research archive, the secondary part

This file holds the secondary part of the M+ catalogue of Suenn Ho's (何巽) research archive
on Kowloon Walled City (九龍城寨). That part is the description of the footage on the fonds
page, and one guess on an item page. M+ wrote the two texts after the gift of 2015. M+ was not
a witness of the City. The footage, the route maps, the catalogue data of each object and the
house numbers on the base plan are primary. They are in the twin file
[`../../primary-sources/mplus-suenn-ho-ca17/SOURCE.md`](../../primary-sources/mplus-suenn-ho-ca17/SOURCE.md).

## Why this is secondary

The fonds description names the gift of 2015. Thus M+ wrote it in 2015 or later, more than 20
years after the clearance. The writer does not say that they saw the City. The text tells what
the footage shows, in the words of M+. Thus it is a caption written later, and an opinion of
the footage. The footage itself is primary. The dates, titles, sizes and lengths of the
objects are catalogue data. They stay in the twin.

## What it rests on

- The cassettes and the maps, which M+ holds. The text does not say that the writer watched
  all the cassettes.
- The labels of the cassettes. The page of CA17/1/3 quotes the cassette label: "Demolition -
  Bristol" and "Demolition - Sydney".
- Suenn Ho, the donor (INFERRED). The text gives facts that only she or her papers can give:
  "her ten-month research on the Kowloon Walled City between 1989 and 1992", and the supply of
  footage to the Wall Street Journal Asia (《亞洲華爾街日報》) for 2014.
- The text says that Ho did not walk alone: "the routes taken by Ho and her research team
  through the Kowloon Walled City". It does not name the team.
- The text gives its view of the worth of the footage: "one of the more extensive video
  documentations of the Kowloon Walled City’s internal spatial configuration, as well as its
  social and physical conditions".
- The text names no other source.

## Limits

- The text gives no cassette, no time code and no place for each scene. Thus a detail such as
  the dripping water has no date and no address.
- Words such as "dingy", "bustling", "voyeuristic", "tight-knit" and "vitality" are the
  opinion of M+. They give no count and no measure.
- The text dates the research "between 1989 and 1992". The catalogue dates the fonds
  1988–1992. The 1988 cassette (CA17/1/3) holds the RTHK (香港電台) documentary. The 1989
  footage on CA17/1/6 is by Po-Chih Leong (梁普智), not by Suenn Ho. Ho's own dated footage
  is from Oct 1991 to 3 Mar 1992 (twin, table of the cassettes).
- The text says "before the city’s complete demolition in 1993". A caption in *City of
  Darkness Revisited* (2014), p347, gives "the whole demolition taking over a year from January
  1993 to March 1994" (see `../../primary-sources/city-of-darkness-revisited/SOURCE.md`). In
  1988 the Governor said "Clearance and demolition will be completed in 1992" (LegCo (立法局)
  Hansard, 12 Oct 1988, in `../../primary-sources/legco-hansard-1986-1993/SOURCE.md`). That
  was a plan, not a record of the end. The demolition started in 1993, but by the p347 caption
  it ended in 1994. Thus "complete demolition in 1993" is doubtful. Use the primary records of
  the demolition.
- The text says that recordings of the Hong Kong Academy for Performing Arts (香港演藝學院)
  (1989) are in the set. No item page names the Academy. The 1989 footage on CA17/1/6 is by
  Po-Chih Leong. The text does not say if the two are the same footage. Do not guess.
- The text calls Michael Chang and Liang Tao (梁濤) "researchers". The pages of CA17/1/7 and
  CA17/1/10 call Michael Chang "a local feng shui expert". Their interviews (1991) can
  hold later history as well as testimony.
- The text names the book as "The City of Darkness: Life in Kowloon Walled City". The title
  of the 1993 book has no "The" (see `../../primary-sources/README.md`).
- The page of CA17/1/11 writes "possibly" for the phase of the clearance on 3 Mar 1992. It is
  a guess of the cataloguer.

## Leads to primary sources

- The 12 VHS cassettes, at the M+ Research Centre, by appointment. The twin lists them. They
  can give a date and a place for each scene in the text. The scenes are the dripping water,
  the shops, the food manufacturers, the aeroplanes over the roofs and the words of the
  residents.
- The recordings of the Hong Kong Academy for Performing Arts (香港演藝學院), 1989. The text
  says that the set includes them. The repo does not hold them. Cassette 5 is not listed
  online (twin). Ask M+ which cassette has them.
- The RTHK (香港電台) documentary "Kowloon Walled City Stories" (九龍城寨的故事) (1988), on
  CA17/1/3. `../../primary-sources/README.md` lists it as a lead.
- The 2014 online exhibition of the Wall Street Journal Asia (《亞洲華爾街日報》). It has
  clips of this footage under "Take a Walk", for example "Look at a well" and "Candy factory".
  See `../../primary-sources/wsj-kwc-2014/SOURCE.md`.
- *City of Darkness: Life in Kowloon Walled City* (1993), by Ian Lambot and Greg Girard. The
  text names it beside the footage. The repo holds the 2014 edition, in
  `../../primary-sources/city-of-darkness-revisited/`.
- The date of the end of the demolition: the *Sing Pao Daily News* (成報) photos of 1987–1994
  in *City of Darkness Revisited*, pp337–347, and the LegCo Hansard of 1986–1993.
- The phase of the clearance near 3 Mar 1992: the cassette CA17/1/11 itself, and the
  newspaper clippings in `../../primary-sources/hku-newspaper-clippings/`.
