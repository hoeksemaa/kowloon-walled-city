# HK01 (香港01), 【真．城寨英雄】 series (2016), the secondary part

26–28 Aug 2016, about Kowloon Walled City (九龍城寨). That part is the text of

## Why this is secondary

The reporter wrote in 2016, more than 20 years after the clearance. She does
not say that she saw the City. Her text has three kinds of part:

1. The words of a resident: in quotes, or in her own words with the name of
   the resident ("葉德偉解釋", "劉太說", "老街坊則認為"). These parts are
   primary. They stay in the twin file.
2. Her own background: history, numbers and descriptions with no named
   speaker. These parts are secondary. They are in this file.
3. The captions. Most photos are the residents' own old photos
   ("相片由受訪者提供"). A photo is a record of its time, but its caption is the
   reporter's text of 2016. A caption that gives the words of a resident stays
   in the twin file ("劉太(左)坦言…"). The other captions are in this file.

## Limits

- The figures of the peak are higher than the official figures. The text
  gives "8000多戶" and "4萬多" people. The Governor's address in LegCo (立法局)
  of 12 Oct 1988 gives "the original 33,000 residents" (see
  [`../../primary-sources/legco-hansard-1986-1993/`](../../primary-sources/legco-hansard-1986-1993/SOURCE.md)).
  Fionnuala McHugh's essay of 2014 gives "28,200 occupants residing in 8800
  separate apartments" (*City of Darkness Revisited*, p340, see
  [`../../primary-sources/city-of-darkness-revisited/`](../../primary-sources/city-of-darkness-revisited/SOURCE.md)).
- The text gives the cordon team of 14 Jan 1987 as "百多人" [over a hundred
  people]. McHugh (p339) gives 360 staff of the Housing Department (房屋署)
  and "nearly an equal number of policemen".
- The text puts the three phases of the clearance "1987至1991年間" [between
  1987 and 1991]. But the last holdouts stayed into July 1992. *The Baltimore
  Sun* of 30 Jun 1992 found "about 100 holdouts" (see
  [`../../primary-sources/press-articles-1959-1992/`](../../primary-sources/press-articles-1959-1992/SOURCE.md)).
  McHugh (p346) gives the police operation of 1 July 1992.
- The text dates the forced entry "1991年11月28日". The LegCo Hansard of 27 Nov
  1991 says: "The Phase I of the Kowloon Walled City clearance exercise is
  going to be carried out today." The two dates differ by one day.
- "七、八十年，城寨多為一、兩層的石屋、鐵皮屋" is not clear. "七、八十年" can mean
  "70 or 80 years ago" or "the 1970s and 1980s". The paragraph tells of the
  childhood of 劉先生, from about 1949. INFERRED: it means the time before the
  high-rises, not the 1970s and 1980s.
- The text gives no date for the first years with no water ("初期"). It gives
  no date ("當時") and no place for the 華聲 strip club.
- The article uses "福利會" for two bodies. A caption in part 1 says that the
  five formed the residents' welfare association (九龍城寨街坊福利會) after they
  left the City. Part 2 gives the work
  on meters and cables to "街坊福利會", thus to the association inside the City.
  The repo calls that body the Kai Fong Association (九龍城砦街坊福利事業促進委員會).
- The history of 1898 is short and simple ("後來英國單方面宣布城寨為殖民地"). Use
  a history work for the question of sovereignty.
- Part 1 dates the change of the free school to the 1970s
  ("於七十年代被改建成義學中心"). Fact Z13 of `data/room_research.json`
  (secondary) says that the Lung Tsun Free School (龍津義學) closed in 1975. It
  says that the high-rise Free School Building (義學大樓) at 5 Lung Chun Road
  (龍津道) took its place.
- The lead of part 2 prints "鼓油". The body prints "豉油" (soy sauce), the
  correct word.
- The twin file keeps the night watch (打更隊), because part 1 gives it to the
  residents ("老街坊則認為"). Its shout "小心火警" comes from the text of part 2,
  in the paragraph about 劉先生.

- Narration: the 義學中心 [free-school centre], 青年中心 [youth centre] and

- Narration (part 2), about the early years: no water supply. People carried

  [meters] in each flat, and helped run the cables.
- Narration: TV aerial cables, phone lines and plastic water pipes filled the
  lanes. A broken pipe was not repaired: "只會再駁一條新的" [they just connected a
  new one].
- Photo caption: "日光之下，城寨之內，仍然非常昏暗" [even in daylight, inside the
  city it was still very dark].

**Numbers (narration, not checked).** "最高峰時住了8000多戶，人口竟高達4萬多" [at
the peak, over 8,000 households, over 40,000 people], "多達20條街道" [as many
