# M+ photographs by Greg Girard and Ian Lambot: the later M+ texts

This file holds the secondary parts of the M+ pages of eight photos of Kowloon Walled City
(九龍城寨) by Greg Girard and Ian Lambot. These parts are the words that M+ wrote about the
City years after the clearance. They are the catalogue texts of two objects, the image
descriptions of all eight objects, and the narration of two audio guides for object 2013.143.
The photos, the catalogue data, the files and their checksums are primary. They are in the
twin file
[`../../primary-sources/mplus-girard-lambot-photos/SOURCE.md`](../../primary-sources/mplus-girard-lambot-photos/SOURCE.md).

- **Citation:** M+, Hong Kong (香港), M+ Collection, the object pages of 2013.143, 2017.410,
  2017.411, 2017.412, 2018.1, 2018.2, 2018.3 and 2018.4. Only two of them have a text about
  the City: 2013.143 (Ian Lambot, "Kowloon Walled City—Aerial View from the Southwest"
  (九龍城寨──西南面鳥瞰圖), 1989, printed 2013) and 2018.4 (Greg Girard, "Kowloon Walled
  City—Tung Tau Tsuen Road facade" (九龍城寨──東頭村道建築外牆), 1987, printed 2015). Each
  text is in English and in Chinese. Each of the eight pages also has a short description of
  its image, in English and in Chinese. The page of 2013.143 also has the M+ audio guides 8038
  and 9038, in English, Cantonese and Mandarin. M+ gives the names of the photographers in
  Chinese as 林保賢 (Lambot) and 格雷格．吉拉德 (Girard). No text names its writer.

  The audio guides: https://www.mplus.org.hk/en/audio-guide/8038-english/ and
  https://www.mplus.org.hk/en/audio-guide/9038-english/ .
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-29 (the saved pages in the twin folder). On 2026-11-16 I decoded the
  page data of the eight saved pages and read all the text in them. On the same day I read
  the live page of 2013.143 and the two English audio guides again. The parts that I checked

## Why this is secondary

M+ wrote these texts years after the clearance. The page of 2013.143 lists two exhibitions:
"Building M+: The Museum and Architecture Collection" (10 January to 9 February 2014) and
"Things, Spaces, Interactions" (12 November 2021 to 2 August 2027). The writers of M+ were not
witnesses of the City. Thus their words are secondary.

One part of audio guide 8038 is not secondary. Chan Cheung Chuen (陳祥全), a former resident,
speaks in it. His words are first-hand testimony, "retrospective (after 2021)". Their main
points are in
[`../../primary-sources/retrospective-testimony-web/SOURCE.md`](../../primary-sources/retrospective-testimony-web/SOURCE.md),
and `docs/people.md` uses them. The primary twin lists his other details. This file keeps only
the words of the presenter of 8038.

## What it rests on

- The catalogue text names one base: "the photographs and oral testimonies gathered in City of
  Darkness". It gives no page and no other source. The repo holds the 2014 edition of the
  book: [`../../primary-sources/city-of-darkness-revisited/SOURCE.md`](../../primary-sources/city-of-darkness-revisited/SOURCE.md).
- The numbers (35,000 people, 350 buildings, 10 to 14 storeys) are round. The text names no
  count and no survey.
- The narration of 8038 rests on the general history of the City and on the interview with
  Chan Cheung Chuen (陳祥全).
- Audio guide 9038 is a spoken description of photo 2013.143. It rests on the photo itself.
  It starts with the title, the medium and the size of the print. INFERRED: it is a
  description for visitors who cannot see the photo.
- The image descriptions rest on the photos only. Each tells what its photo shows, in two or
  three sentences.

## Limits

- No text gives its writer or its date.
- The English text of 2013.143 names only "British occupation". The text of 2018.4 names
  "both British and Japanese occupation". The Chinese text of 2013.143 also names the two
  ("於英治和日佔時期").
- "one square city block" is a figure of speech. Audio guide 9038 calls the City "one big
  rectangular city block".
- "over thirty-five thousand people" is more than the registration of 14–15 January 1987
  counted: "just over 33,000", with the day workers (book, p339). The book also gives "35,000
  or so" (p249). See `../../primary-sources/city-of-darkness-revisited/SOURCE.md`.
- "approximately 350 buildings": the model has 277 buildings. `data/room_research.json`
  records this disagreement ("Number of buildings"). The book gives "some 300 individual
  buildings" (p340).
- "ten to fourteen storeys": the book gives "up to a possible 15 storeys" for the buildings
  (p340). The model gives 15 storeys to one narrow strip (see `docs/structure.md`).
- Audio guide 8038 dates the photo "in the 1980s". The catalogue and 9038 give 1989.
- The presenter of 8038 says that the City is "full of childhood memories" for Chan Cheung
  Chuen (陳祥全). But Chan says that he lived in Tung Tau Estate (東頭邨) as a child. He moved
  into the City after he married.
- The English and the Cantonese narration of 8038 are not the same text. Only the Cantonese
  says that the Qing (清朝) court held to its sovereignty. Only the Cantonese says that the
  City had no official planning.
- The text gives no source for the year 1985 of the clearance of Sai Tau Tsuen (西頭村). The
  survey sheet of May 1987 marks "CIP June 1986" on its ground (see `data/surroundings.json`).
  This agrees with 1985. But `docs/people.md` tells, from the interview with Lam Leung Po
  (林良寶) (book, p292), that his factory was in Sai Tau Tsuen "until the village came down".
  He joined it in 1983, after it moved into the City. INFERRED: part of the village came down
  before 1983.
- Audio guide 9038 uses the name "Carpenter Road Park" (賈炳達道公園). Its Cantonese text says
  that the park was "當時仍然一片荒蕪" (still bare at the time). The name can be later than
  1989.
- Audio guide 9038 uses the later name "Former Yamen Building". Its Cantonese text says
  "以前清朝嘅衙門" (the old Qing yamen). The repo calls it the Yamen (衙門).
- Audio guide 9038 gives "eight to twelve storeys" for the public housing blocks. It names no
  estate. `tools/context_input.json` gives 16 storeys for Tung Tau Estate Block 23
  (東頭邨第23座). Thus check each block on the photo before you use the number.
- The English and the Cantonese of 9038 count the housing blocks in two ways. The English
  gives "around six residential blocks". The Cantonese gives "五、六組樓房" (five or six
  groups), each group of two or three buildings.

## Leads to primary sources

- *City of Darkness* (1993): the M+ text names it as its base. The repo holds the 2014
  edition, with its photos, interviews and documents (link above). Use them before the M+
  numbers.
- The clearance of Sai Tau Tsuen (西頭村): the M+ text gives 1985 and no source. The GRS
  (政府檔案處) files and the LegCo (立法局) Hansard of the time can give the date. On
  2026-11-16 I found no "Sai Tau" in `../../primary-sources/grs-catalogue-kwc/` or in
  `../../primary-sources/legco-hansard-1986-1993/`. A new GRS search for "Sai Tau Tsuen" can
  find clearance files.
- The map of the streets and the upper walkways by Chan Cheung Chuen (陳祥全), named in
  audio guide 8038. `../../primary-sources/README.md` already lists it as a lead.
- M+ Magazine, Lap-wai Lam, "City of Innocence", 27 Apr 2023, on Girard's photos:
  https://www.mplus.org.hk/en/magazine/exploring-kowloon-walled-city-photographic-journey/ .
  It can quote Girard (retrospective). Its own text is secondary. I did not read it. On
  2026-11-16 a check of the live page confirmed only its title, its writer and its date.

The twin had this section, "Facts in the M+ text". The split changed one sentence to the active

thousand people in one square city block", with "approximately 350 buildings", "towers ten
to fourteen storeys high". M+ wrote this text in the 2010s, thus it is not primary. The photos
are the primary part.
