### top-commons-22: Europe in China/Chapter 17

- **Creator:** Ernst Johann Eitel; transcribed on English Wikisource
- **Date:** 1895
- **Kind:** book
- **Class:** secondary (A history written about forty years after the events of 1854.)
- **Value:** low
- **Language:** en
- **Access:** open
- **Licence:** public domain (age); Wikisource transcription CC BY-SA 4.0
- **Link:** https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Europe_in_China/Chapter_17
- **Proof that it exists:** Chapter XVII, 'The Administration of Sir John Bowring': 'Kowloon city, opposite Hongkong, was at the end of September, 1854, repeatedly taken and retaken by the Rebels'.
- **What it gives (finder's note, not checked):** Rebels and imperial troops took and took back Kowloon City several times at the end of September 1854. The search extract adds that crowds of fugitives took refuge in Hong Kong.
- **Notes:** The page did not show the year of the book. 1895 is from my own knowledge and is not checked here. The page text is 135,077 characters; WebFetch read the first 100,000. Chapters 9, 10, 19 and 20 also name Kowloon city (1839, the Kowloon City promontory, the customs stations, the ferry attempt of July 1873); not opened.
