### gap-district-board-housing-authority-17: Information guide to Kowloon City District

- **Status:** Partly read on 2026-11-20; a checker checked a sample of the facts on 2026-11-20. The eBook is open: 24 page images through the IIIF viewer (no text layer). It is a bilingual booklet with one page for each government department. I read page 5 (origins of the names), page 13 (district map), the English columns of pages 15 (Medical and Health) and 19 (Police) and page 22 (temporary shelters); I saw the other pages only at low resolution. The City is named once (p. 5); the map does not mark it.
- **Citation:** Hong Kong. Kowloon City District Management Committee, 九龍城民政區資料簡介 / Information Guide to Kowloon City District, 1981, in English and Chinese, 24 page images; gift from the Kowloon City District Office. HKU Libraries eBooks, accession hkulebooks_10792, https://digitalrepository.lib.hku.hk/catalog/wh247z451
- **Class:** primary (A government district guide made at the time (1981). Its remarks on the origins of the names are background (secondary). The primary tree is correct.)
- **Date of the record:** 1981
- **Note:** The secondary part of the item of the same id in [`../../primary-sources/kowloon-city-district-board-1981-1994/SOURCE.md`](../../primary-sources/kowloon-city-district-board-1981-1994/SOURCE.md).
