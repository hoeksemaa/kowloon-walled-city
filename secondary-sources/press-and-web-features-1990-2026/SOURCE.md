- **Proof that it exists:** PDF text: 'A18 Saturday, March 16, 2013 ... City of anarchy ... Sources: The Darkness City: Life in Kowloon Walled City - Greg Girard and Ian Lambot, Leisure and Cultural Services Department   SCMP Graphic: Adolfo Arranz'
- **What it gives (finder's note, not checked):** A cutaway drawing of a block of the City with labelled figures: 500 buildings on 2.7 ha (126 m x 213 m), buildings no higher than 14 floors, 77 wells 'some 90 metres deep' with electric pumps to roof tanks, eight freshwater standpipes (one inside the City), HK$35 monthly room rent, 40 sq ft per person, 'the 700 or so industrial premises' (metal shops between the ground and fifth floors), schools and kindergartens (some by the Salvation Army), rooftop uses, umbrellas against dripping pipes, police could only arrest non-residents. - **Notes:** The SOPA Awards 2014 entry PDF is the printed page A18 of 16 Mar 2013, with selectable text (1 page, 1,446,577 bytes, SHA-256 4bfa84e620a346a4e40b8af3493aff19b0c66aa393268ec5034d1ccddc1a1078 of the copy that WebFetch saved; PDF dated 2013-03-15). The source line miscites the book as 'The Darkness City'. Several figures disagree with primary sources: 50,000 residents against the 1987 survey's about 33,000; '77 wells' against 'eight standpipes'; 500 buildings against the usual 300-350. The drawing is a reconstruction: use it for ideas, not for sizes or counts. ArchDaily (Nicky Rackard, 18 Apr 2013) reposted it as 'Infographic: Life Inside The Kowloon Walled City' and added '250 sq ft or less' per shared flat and alleys '1 to 2 meters wide'.
