The secondary twin of [`../../primary-sources/visitor-photos-1970-1993/SOURCE.md`](../../primary-sources/visitor-photos-1970-1993/SOURCE.md): the narration of
reporters and editors, the later captions and the histories in its items. The files of the source stay
in the primary folder.

  facts of each chunk.

## Limits
