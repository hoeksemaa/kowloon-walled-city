### blocked-b-1: Pre-clearance survey of the Kowloon Walled City, 14 January 1987 (results as quoted in 香港九龍城スラムの空間構成)

- **Status:** Read on 2026-11-19; a checker checked a sample of the facts on 2026-11-19.
- **Citation:** Shunichi J. Watanabe (渡辺俊一) and Masahiro Ibayashi (居林昌宏), 'Spatial Structure of the Kowloon Walled City Slum, Hong Kong' (「香港九龍城スラムの空間構成」), Journal of the City Planning Institute of Japan (『都市計画論文集』), City Planning Institute of Japan (日本都市計画学会), vol. 28, 1993 (issue date 1993-10-25), pp. 439-444, quoting the Hong Kong Government survey of 14 January 1987 and the government's final clearance figures; pp. 440-441; https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/journalcpij/28/0/28_439/_pdf/-char/ja (DOI 10.11361/journalcpij.28.439).
- **Class:** secondary (What can be read is a 1993 secondary paper that quotes the 1987 survey results and the 1992 clearance totals; the survey record itself (primary) is not online. While only the quotation is held, this item belongs in secondary-sources (or merges with the paper's own item); keep a primary entry only if the Housing Department survey report is found.)
- **Date of the record:** Survey: 14 January 1987 (early morning); clearance totals: as of 2 July 1992; paper published 25 October 1993
- **Note:** The secondary part of the item of the same id in [`../../primary-sources/hk-government-records-1899-1996/SOURCE.md`](../../primary-sources/hk-government-records-1899-1996/SOURCE.md).
