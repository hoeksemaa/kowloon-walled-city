# HKU Hong Kong Newspaper Clippings Online: 334 clippings on the Walled City, 1969–1997

Clippings from Hong Kong English-language newspapers, mostly the *South China
Morning Post* and the *Hong Kong Standard*. Dr Norman Miners collected them
for the HKU Department of Political Science. HKU Libraries scanned them.

- **Link:** https://digitalrepository.lib.hku.hk/hkclippings?q=%22walled+city%22
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-29. The search for `"walled city"` gave 334 records.
- **Access:** the catalogue records (title, date, byline) are free. Each
  clipping image has `visibility: authenticated`, thus you must sign in to HKU
  to read it. I did not read any clipping.
- **Copyright:** the newspapers (SCMP, Sing Tao / *Hong Kong Standard*).
- **Stored in git:** only `index.csv`, the catalogue index. There are no images.

## Why this is primary

Each clipping is a newspaper report from the time. Local reporters went into the
city, and many reports quote residents and officials.

## `index.csv`

One row for each record, sorted by date. Columns: `date`, `title`, `byline`,
`images` (number of scanned pages), `accession`, `url` (HKU catalogue page),
`visibility`. SHA-256
`55dc1a72cf24b5ad25787f8588a0d2f2089b3af8a214e0ca1a03cd32bb98e596`.

By year: 1987 has 64 records, 1992 has 47, 1991 has 45, 1989 has 30, and 1993
has 26. Three records have no date. 215 records have a byline.

## How to read the clippings

You must have an HKU Libraries login. Other ways to get the same reports:
the SCMP historical archive (ProQuest), or a library that has the papers on
microfilm.
