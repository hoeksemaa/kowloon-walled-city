### blocked-b-3: 由城寨街坊自行繪製的九龍寨城街道圖 (street map of the Walled City drawn by its own kaifong), reproduced in 《九龍寨城：從邊城到庶民社區的歷史傳奇》 p.81

- **Creator:** Residents (kaifong) of the Walled City, per the caption; reproduced by Hong Kong (中華書局)
- **Date:** c.1987 (INFERRED; the sample gives no date)
- **Kind:** map
- **Class:** primary (A plan of the City drawn by its residents before the demolition, per the caption.)
- **Value:** high
- **Language:** zh
- **Access:** open
- **Licence:** © 中華書局 (sample); the map: unknown
- **Link:** https://www.chunghwabook.com.hk/upload/files/2026/07_09/8961d31fc1bbfcc1dbd8f8cb3e13d50a.pdf
- **Other links:** https://www.mplus.org.hk/en/collection/archives/set-of-maps-relating-to-the-video-footage-kowloon-walled-city-research-project-ca17-2
- **Proof that it exists:** Sample PDF page 6 (book p.81), caption: "由城寨街坊自行繪製的九龍寨城街道圖，可見像迷宮的佈局。"
- **What it gives (finder's note, not checked):** A whole-City plan with Tung Tau Tsuen Road lettered along a curved top edge, the Yamen compound in the centre, building names, and heavy lines with dots that can be the upper walkways. It answers the README question on the maker of the base plan under the Suenn Ho route maps (INFERRED, see notes).
- **Notes:** I compared the p.81 image (rendered at 200 dpi) with the M+ route map CA17/2/3 (1000 px copy in the repo). The two look like the same base plan: the same 'TUNG TAU TSUEN' lettering on the curved road, the same Yamen outline, heavy dotted lines and entrance triangles. This is INFERRED from a visual check, not confirmed. If it is right, the base plan is a residents' map, and it can be Chan Cheung Chuen's map (blocked-b-11), which WSJ says he finished in March 1987. The M+ series page (opened) says only 'Three maps showing the routes taken by Suenn Ho' and names no base plan. The sample shows the map small (about 1000 px wide at 200 dpi); buy the book for a larger copy and the picture credits.
