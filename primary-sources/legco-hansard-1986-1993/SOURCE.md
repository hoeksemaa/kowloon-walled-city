# Hong Kong Legislative Council, Official Record of Proceedings, 1986–1993

The English Hansard of every LegCo sitting from Oct 1986 to Jul 1993 that
names the Walled City. That is 22 sittings out of 251.

- **Publisher:** Legislative Council of Hong Kong, www.legco.gov.hk
- **URL pattern:** `https://www.legco.gov.hk/yr<SESSION>/english/lc_sitg/hansard/h<YYMMDD>.pdf`
  (for example `yr86-87`). The session index pages are
  `https://www.legco.gov.hk/yr<SESSION>/english/lc_sitg/yr<YYYY>.htm`.
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-29. The server gives `Last-Modified` dates of about
  Aug 2005 for these PDFs.
- **Stored in git:** no. The PDFs are in `local/`, which git ignores. Use the
  URL and the SHA-256 below to get the same file again.

## Why this is primary

Hansard is the verbatim record of each sitting. It records what officials and
members said on the day. It gives official figures for the clearance as it
happened.

## What it is good for

It is strong on numbers and dates: residents, families, rehousing,
compensation and clearance phases. It says little about daily life. The
debates mention illegal doctors and dentists, "illegal flats" and the
residents' protests.

## Method

I got all 251 sittings from Oct 1986 to Jul 1993. I searched their text for
"walled city", across line breaks. Two short PDFs (h861210, h930219) have a
text layer, so the search covered them. Chinese Hansard is not yet searched.
