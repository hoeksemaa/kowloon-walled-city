- **Date:** 2025-09-06
- **Kind:** testimony
- **Class:** retrospective (A Japanese student who lived in the City from 1985 tells of his flat and neighbours in 2025.)
- **Value:** high
- **Language:** ja
- **Access:** open
- **Licence:** © Shogakukan
- **Link:** https://www.news-postseven.com/archives/20250906_2060258.html
- **Other links:** https://www.news-postseven.com/archives/20250906_2060258.html/2, https://www.news-postseven.com/archives/20250906_2060258.html/3, https://news.yahoo.co.jp/articles/af28f6416783d2c564501ba74451bfb1a03a6876

- **Value:** medium
- **Language:** ja
- **Access:** open
- **Licence:** © ASCII / KADOKAWA

### ja-7: 激レアさんを連れてきた。 (episode with 吉田一郎: 家賃が安いという理由で九龍城に住んでいた)

- **Creator:** TV Asahi (テレビ朝日); summary page by インフォダイブ (info-dive.net)
- **Date:** 2017-12-25
- **Kind:** testimony
- **Class:** retrospective (A TV programme in which the former resident tells of his stay; only a fan summary of it is online.)
- **Value:** medium
- **Language:** ja
- **Access:** open
- **Licence:** © TV Asahi (programme); © info-dive.net (summary)
- **Link:** https://www.info-dive.net/tv/geki-rare-04
- **Proof that it exists:** Page title: 「激レアさん！吉田一郎 家賃が安いという理由で九龍城に住んでいた」 (インフォダイブ), 2017年12月25日
- **What it gives (finder's note, not checked):** A corner room on the 14th floor: 「14階の角部屋だったため、2分に1本、飛行機が飛んでうるさかった」; foul water fell from above (「降り注ぐ汚水が臭かった」); rent about a quarter of the Hong Kong rate, about 10,000 yen a month; a stay of about five months. He left when the government blocked the drain to push out the wooden houses in front of the City, and sewage pooled on his way to school.
- **Notes:** A summary by a third party, not a transcript: the figures differ from ja-3 (15,000 yen, 2LDK). The broadcast itself was not found online. The blocked drain fits the clearance of the squatter area round the City (Sai Tau Tsuen) in the mid-1980s: INFERRED, check it.

- **Creator:** Shinchosha (デイリー新潮); testimony of 吉田一郎
- **Date:** 2024-05-26
- **Kind:** testimony
- **Class:** retrospective (A former resident of the mid-1980s tells of his building and the clearance in 2024.)

- **Link:** https://www.dailyshincho.jp/article/2024/05261049/?all=1

- **Proof that it exists:** First sentence: 「日本で「九龍城」「九龍城砦」、香港で「九龍城寨」と呼ばれた大型スラム街を覚えているだろうか。」
- **What it gives (finder's note, not checked):** Slippery floors, 「ぬるぬるの理由はほとんどが汚水」; broken drains left as they were (「排水管が壊れても修理できないので放置するしかなかった」); little community life for him: he knew the estate agent and 「部屋の両隣りの人」.
