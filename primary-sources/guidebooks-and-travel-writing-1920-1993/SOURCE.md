### gap-guidebooks-travel-writing-7: Kowloon Walled City (in Friends Bulletin, Pacific Yearly Meeting of the Religious Society of Friends, April-May 1971)

- **Creator:** Madge Seaver
- **Date:** 1971-04
- **Kind:** article
- **Class:** primary (A visitor's own report of a guided visit, written at the time.)
- **Value:** medium
- **Language:** en
- **Access:** open
- **Licence:** unknown (archive.org collection 'westernfriend'; no rights statement)
- **Link:** https://archive.org/details/friendsbulletin3978unse_2
- **Other links:** https://archive.org/stream/friendsbulletin3978unse_2/friendsbulletin3978unse_2_djvu.txt
- **Proof that it exists:** Friends Bulletin, April-May 1971, pp. 9-10: 'We went into the Kowloon Walled City with many preconceptions and misconceptions.'
- **What it gives (finder's note, not checked):** A Quaker visit guided by Barbara Taylor of the Hong Kong Meeting: entry down steps past a sagging fence and barbed wire, open drains, dim covered lanes, ironworks, flour mills, weaving sheds and bakeries; dentists with false teeth in the windows; water only from standpipes round the edge. It describes Maureen Clark's Child Care Centre and kindergarten (about 40 children; baby clinic three mornings a week).
- **Notes:** Earlier than the peak (1971). The repo has the Child Care Centre (data/room_research.json, 'Child Care Centre (Maureen Clarke)', from CoDR p263); this source spells the name 'Maureen Clark'. I read the djvu text through a summary tool: check the exact words before you quote them.
