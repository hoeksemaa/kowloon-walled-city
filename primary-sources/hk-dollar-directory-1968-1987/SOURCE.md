# Hong Kong Dollar Directory, 1968-1987

Yearly directories with City clinics, schools and missions; open on archive.org.

- **Status:** catalogued on 2026-11-17 by the source search of 2026-11. Nobody has read these items for the repo yet.
  "What it gives" is the short note of the agent that found the item. It is not a checked fact.
  Before you use a fact in the model, read it on the source and write it in this file with its
  page and a short exact quote.
- **Dates:** 1968-1987.
- **Items:** 4, of which 4 are open now. Value of the set: high.
- **Copyright:** see the licence of each item. Copyrighted files go in `local/`, which git ignores.
- **Stored in git:** this file only.

Each item says its class and the reason. An item that is "retrospective" is first-hand

### top-missions-1: Hong Kong $ directory (1983): The Salvation Army, Hong Kong Command (Educational Department), schools and kindergartens, p. 920; clinics, p. 995

- **Creator:** Local Printing Press (publisher); scan by RCL Canada-Hong Kong Library, University of Toronto
- **Date:** 1983
- **Kind:** data
- **Class:** primary (A directory made in 1983 from the returns of each body; it lists the Salvation Army units inside the City at that time.)
- **Value:** high
- **Language:** en
- **Access:** open
- **Licence:** © publisher (Local Printing Press); University of Toronto scan; no rights statement on the item
- **Link:** https://archive.org/details/31761123931974
- **Other links:** https://archive.org/details/31761123931974/page/n1025/mode/1up, https://archive.org/details/31761123931974/page/n1104/mode/1up, https://archive.org/download/31761123931974/31761123931974_djvu.txt
- **Proof that it exists:** p. 920: 'SALVATION ARMY, THE / Hong Kong Command. (Educational Department). 547, Nathan Road, Kowloon'
- **What it gives (finder's note, not checked):** Salvation Army (救世軍) units in the City in 1983: 'Pre-School Educational Centre. 14, Loong Chun Road, Walled City. Kowloon. Tel. 3-834435' and, under Kindergartens, 'Walled City. 14, Loong Chun Road, Walled City Kowloon City, Kowloon. Tel. 3-834435' (p. 920). Under CLINICS (p. 995): 'Walled City Clinic. 28 Lung Chung Road, G/F., Walled City, Kowloon. Tel. 3-820644', with 'Supervisor—Major Glen Gilden' and 'Senior Medical Doctor—Dr. Yen Yee Hang, M.D.'
- **Notes:** I read the page images of leaves n1025 (printed p. 920) and n1104 (printed p. 995). The heading 'SALVATION ARMY, THE' is on p. 920; the clinics on p. 995 are in the same section (the same nurseries, creches and Ma Wan camp follow on p. 996), but the section heading is on an earlier page: INFERRED that the clinic is the Salvation Army's. 'Loong Chun Road' is the directory's spelling of Lung Chun Road (龍津道, the repo's name). 'Lung Chung Road' is the directory's spelling; which street of the City it means is not certain. The street lens (gap-street-address-search) searched this directory only for 'Lung Chun Road' and similar spellings and found no City address: 'Loong Chun' and 'Lung Chung' were not searched. The archive.org full-text API (service_backend=fts, collection canadahongkonglibrary) finds these entries.

### top-missions-2: Hong Kong $ directory (1987): The Salvation Army, kindergartens, p. 885; clinics, p. 968

- **Creator:** Local Printing Press (publisher); scan by RCL Canada-Hong Kong Library, University of Toronto
- **Date:** 1987
- **Kind:** data
- **Class:** primary (A directory made at the time; it is the edition nearest to the model week of December 1986.)
- **Value:** high
- **Language:** en
- **Access:** open
- **Licence:** © publisher (Local Printing Press); University of Toronto scan; no rights statement on the item
- **Link:** https://archive.org/details/31761120250253
- **Other links:** https://archive.org/details/31761120250253/page/n969/mode/1up, https://archive.org/details/31761120250253/page/n886/mode/1up, https://archive.org/details/31761120250246
- **Proof that it exists:** p. 968: 'CLINICS / Supervisor—Major Glen Gilden. / Senior Medical Doctor— Dr. Yen Yee Hang, M.D.'
- **What it gives (finder's note, not checked):** The Salvation Army 'Walled City Clinic. 28 Lung Chung Road, G/F., Walled City, Kowloon. Tel. 3-820644' is still listed in 1987 (p. 968), with the same supervisor and senior doctor as in 1983. The kindergarten list (p. 885) goes from Sau Mau Ping to Jat Min Chuen with no 'Walled City' entry, and an in-item search finds no 'Pre-School Educational' and no 'Chun Road': the kindergarten and the pre-school centre at 14 Loong Chun Road are no longer listed.
- **Notes:** I read the page images of leaves n969 (printed p. 968) and n886 (printed p. 885). The reverse of p. 968 shows through with 'SALVATION ARMY' and 'HOMES/HOSTEL FOR THE AGED': the clinics are in the Salvation Army section (heading on an earlier page, INFERRED). The date of the data in the 1987 edition is not stated: the end of the kindergarten can be any time between the 1983 data and the 1987 data. The 1985 edition (alt URL, 31761120250246) also lists the Walled City Clinic at 28 Lung Chung Road (full-text search extract only; not read). The Salvation Army Year Book of 1985 still lists the kindergarten (see top-missions-3).

### top-missions-5: Hong Kong $ directory (1971), Hong Kong Council of Social Service, 'Societies affiliated to the Council': 'Child Care Centre—Walled City', p. 1222

- **Creator:** Local Printing Press (publisher); scan by RCL Canada-Hong Kong Library, University of Toronto
- **Date:** 1971
- **Kind:** data
- **Class:** primary (A directory list of 1971 of the welfare bodies that belonged to the Council of Social Service.)
- **Value:** medium
- **Language:** en
- **Access:** open
- **Licence:** © publisher (Local Printing Press); University of Toronto scan; no rights statement on the item
- **Link:** https://archive.org/details/31761120250287/page/n1317/mode/1up
- **Other links:** https://archive.org/details/31761120250287
- **Proof that it exists:** p. 1222: 'HONG KONG COUNCIL OF SOCIAL SERVICE, THE / Anne Black Red Cross Building, 2nd Floor, Harcourt Road'
- **What it gives (finder's note, not checked):** In 1971 a body named 'Child Care Centre—Walled City' was a society affiliated to the Hong Kong Council of Social Service, in the same list as the Boys' & Girls' Clubs Association, Caritas—Hong Kong, Hong Kong Christian Service and the Christian Children's Fund, Inc. It gives no address or operator.
- **Notes:** I read the page image (leaf n1317, printed p. 1222). The next line is 'Christian Children's Fund, Inc.', a separate entry: the list does not say who ran the child care centre. A lead: the Christian Children's Fund had a South China office under Rev. E. W. Raetz (The China Press, 19 Apr 1949, search extract), and Erwin Raetz worked with the Donnithornes in the City (World Vision, Feb 1969, top-missions-20). Thus the operator can be the CCF or the West China Evangelistic Band: not known. The same 1971 edition has Salvation Army entries for the Walled City on pp. 1235 and 1263 (in-item search).
