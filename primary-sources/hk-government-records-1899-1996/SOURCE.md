# Hong Kong government reports and records, 1899-1996

Annual reports, audit, police, housing, airport height order, laws and court records of the time.

- **Status:** partly read: 16 of 17 items read by the reading of the source search of
  2026-11, and a checker checked a sample of the facts. Each other item is still catalogued, not
  read: its "What it gives" is the note of the finder, not a checked fact.
- **Dates:** 1899-2001.
- **Items:** 17, of which 9 are open now. Value of the set: high.
- **Copyright:** see the licence of each item. Copyrighted files go in `local/`, which git ignores.
- **Stored in git:** this file only.

## Why this is primary

Each item says its class and the reason. An item that is "retrospective" is first-hand
testimony given later: tag its facts "retrospective (year)".
