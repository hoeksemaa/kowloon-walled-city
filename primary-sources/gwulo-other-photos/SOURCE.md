## 19655: dentist's shop, about 1987/88

- Caption: "Dentist on the ground floor of the former Kowloon Walled city -
  taken around 1987/88".
- The image is mirrored on purpose. A commenter asked Gwulo to flip it so that
  the Chinese characters read correctly, and Gwulo replaced the file. The
  original upload is no longer on the server (404). Server `Last-Modified`: 20
  Oct 2014.
- What it shows: a dental chair, an overhead light, glass shelves, pink wall
  tiles, a terrazzo floor and a shop sign "凌生牙科" over the room.
- **Probable match:** GRS case file HKRS1255-3-34 is "G/F with a cockloft, 94
  Lung Chun Road Cheng Ling Sang Dentistry (鄭凌生牙科)" (see
  `../grs-catalogue-kwc/`). The sign in the photo can be the same shop. I
  did not confirm it.
