### books-9: Rupert & Fanny's Big Bike Trip: "Royal Hong Kong Police – The streets of Kowloon" (chapter 2 of a serial memoir)

- **Creator:** Blog user 'rupertutley', a former inspector of the Royal Hong Kong Police
- **Date:** 2021-06-24
- **Kind:** testimony
- **Class:** retrospective (A former police inspector's own memoir of his patrols from Kowloon City Police Station in the late 1980s, written in 2021.)
- **Value:** high
- **Language:** en
- **Access:** open
- **Licence:** © the author (blog)
- **Link:** https://bigbiketrip.net/2021/06/24/royal-hong-kong-police-chapter-2/
- **Other links:** https://bigbiketrip.net/2021/06/23/royal-hong-kong-police-chapter-1/
- **Proof that it exists:** First sentence: "Although recipient of “Baton of Honour” when passing out of Police Training Schools, I was posted to Kowloon City…"; posted 24 Jun 2021, updated 9 Oct 2024.
- **What it gives (finder's note, not checked):** A patrol sub-unit officer on foot at Kowloon City: he often climbed onto the roof to watch airliners pass to Kai Tak; huge rats in the alleys; rubbish between the tenements; fish paste for fish balls left to ferment on the bare ground; illegal dentists on the outside of the block; hundreds of people; children in 'immaculately clean white uniforms' going to school. Before his SDU selection of Nov 1988, thus about 1986-88 (INFERRED).
- **Notes:** The en-testimony finder has chapter 1 only (en-testimony-13); chapter 2 holds the Walled City passages. The page gives no exact year of the Kowloon City posting. The full name is not on the text that I read (the user name is 'rupertutley'). Check later chapters for more.

### en-testimony-7: The '633' Squad (in Stories from the Royal Hong Kong Police: Fifty accounts from officers of Hong Kong's colonial-era police force)

- **Creator:** John Murray (former RHKP inspector); The Royal Hong Kong Police Association; Blacksmith Books
- **Date:** 2020
- **Kind:** book
- **Class:** retrospective (A former police officer tells of a raid in the City that he took part in, in 1973.)
- **Value:** medium
- **Language:** en
- **Access:** open
- **Licence:** © 2020 the contributors (publisher's free preview)
- **Link:** https://www.blacksmithbooks.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/Stories_from_the_RHKP_preview.pdf
- **Other links:** https://pastforward.org/products/stories-from-the-royal-hong-kong-police-fifty-accounts-from-officers-of-hong-kongs-colonial-era-police-force-9789887963882
- **Proof that it exists:** Later that month we carried out a raid that became quite famous locally and could have had political consequences.
- **What it gives (finder's note, not checked):** pp. 156-162: Murray left Police Training School in mid-October 1973 and joined a raid squad. Late in November 1973 the squad raided a heroin divan in the City with a warrant that 'had no legal standing'; Superintendent Ko said that Governor MacLehose had sanctioned it to test China's reaction. Beijing's reaction was nil, which opened the way for further raids. He also says most ground-floor premises were dentists qualified in China.
- **Notes:** ISBN 978-988-79638-8-2. The free preview PDF holds the whole chapter (I read it with pdftotext). The other 49 chapters were not checked for the City. Earlier history (1973), but it dates the start of police raids inside the City.

### en-testimony-13: Royal Hong Kong Police – The beginning

- **Creator:** Rupert Utley ('rupertutley'), blog bigbiketrip.net
- **Date:** 2021-06-23
- **Kind:** testimony
- **Class:** retrospective (A former RHKP inspector tells of his first attachment to Kowloon City Division in 1987.)
- **Value:** low
- **Language:** en
- **Access:** open
- **Licence:** © the author
- **Link:** https://bigbiketrip.net/2021/06/23/royal-hong-kong-police-chapter-1/
- **Proof that it exists:** The goldsmith robbery getaway car came screeching into the carpark of Hung Hom ferry pier with Car 8 from Mong Kok in hot pursuit.
- **What it gives (finder's note, not checked):** He joined the RHKP on 19 Feb 1987 and passed out on 14 Nov 1987; his attachment in the week before was to 'Kowloon City Division, of "Walled City" fame'. The Divisional Commander was Superintendent Paul Deal, the only other expatriate in the station. His plane in 1987 'skimmed over the roof of the infamous Walled City'. Little on the City itself.
- **Notes:** Useful for the police of the model period (late 1987): the name of the Kowloon City divisional commander. Later chapters of the blog were not checked.

### top-books-15: Elsie Tu Personal Papers

- **Creator:** Hong Kong Baptist University Library, Special Collections and Archives
- **Date:** c.1950-2015
- **Kind:** catalogue
- **Class:** primary (Her own papers: manuscripts, letters, photos, clippings, and reports and committee papers of government departments.)
- **Value:** low
- **Language:** en
- **Access:** offline
- **Licence:** © HKBU Library / the estate of Elsie Tu
- **Link:** https://library.hkbu.edu.hk/collections/special-collections-archives/personal-papers/elsie-tu-papers/
- **Other links:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsie_Tu
- **Proof that it exists:** Page: "Elsie Tu Personal Papers" (HKBU Library Special and Archival Collections), last updated 07 November 2024.
- **What it gives (finder's note, not checked):** Not confirmed for the City. The page names the Star Ferry riots of 1966, anti-corruption work, Patten's reforms and the MacLennan case. She sat on the Urban Council (1963-95) and LegCo (1988-95), thus her committee papers can cover the clearance years.
- **Notes:** No online finding aid. Access by request to HKBU Special Collections and Archives. Wikipedia (from HK Magazine, 2 Mar 2006) says that she lived for a time in a kitchen in a Walled City tenement (books-17 has this). Ask the archive for files on the Walled City or Kowloon City.
