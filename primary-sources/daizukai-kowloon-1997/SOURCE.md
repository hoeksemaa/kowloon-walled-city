# 『大図解 九龍城』 (Iwanami Shoten, 1997): the panorama labels

A Japanese book with a large cutaway panorama of the City. Labels in the
panorama name the use of many rooms. A Japanese team, the 九龍城探検隊,
surveyed the empty City in early 1993, and the book publishes their work. Only
part of the panorama is on the open web.

- **Citation:** 九龍城探検隊 (text), 可児弘明 Kani Hiroaki (supervisor, 監修),
  寺澤一美 Terasawa Hitomi (drawings), 『大図解 九龍城』. 岩波書店 Iwanami
  Shoten, 10 Jul 1997. ISBN 9784000080705.
- **Link:** https://www.rioleo.org/kowloon/ ("The Kowloon Walled City panorama
  (with English annotations)"). The page has no date and no author name. It
  shows the panorama as `crosssection.jpg`. The book:
  https://www.kinokuniya.co.jp/f/dsg-01-9784000080705
- **Retrieved:** 2026-11-05. An agent read the Japanese labels in
  `crosssection.jpg` at full size. The English notes of the page miss several
  rooms. I did not save the image.
- **Copyright:** © the artist and Iwanami Shoten. The rioleo.org page gives no
  license. It says that the drawings "owe [their] existence to Terasawa Hitomi,
  who illustrated the pages in the book Daizukan Kyuryujyou".
- **Stored in git:** no. This folder holds only this file.

## Which parts are primary

- **The survey: primary for rooms, not for life.** The 九龍城探検隊, led by
  鈴木隆行 Takayuki Suzuki, measured a slice of the City after the residents
  left. *City of Darkness Revisited* (pp208–209) reproduces some of their
  photos, sketches and plans. It dates the survey "January 1993" (p209) and
  "February 1993" (p204). See `../city-of-darkness-revisited/`, facts M89–M93.
  The survey records the rooms, the stairs and what the empty rooms held.
- **The panorama: a reconstruction.** The artist filled the rooms with people
  and trades, as the City was before the clearance. The book does not say
  which label comes from the survey and which comes from stories. Some labels
  tell a story, for example a triad office with strip joints on the floors
  above and below (Z46). Thus every label has the basis "guess". Use the labels
  for the kinds of rooms and their mix, not for the address of one room.
- The same artist drew the fold-out of *City of Darkness Revisited* (© 2014
  Hitomi Terasawa). That book says its fold-out "is far from accurate".

## Gaps

- rioleo.org shows only panorama pages 5–6 and 11–12. Pages 7–10 and the rest
  of the book, with the room plans of the survey, are print only. Buy a used
  copy.
- The labels give no house numbers. Japanese floor names count G/F as 1階,
  thus 2階 is 1/F in Hong Kong usage.
