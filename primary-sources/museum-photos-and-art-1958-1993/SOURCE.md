### top-institutions-3: 九龍城の阿片窟 (A Kowloon opium den)

- **Creator:** Nagano Shigeichi (長野重一); Tokyo Photographic Art Museum (東京都写真美術館)
- **Date:** 1958
- **Kind:** photo
- **Class:** primary (A photo made at the time by a photographer who was there.)
- **Value:** low
- **Language:** ja
- **Access:** open
- **Licence:** unknown (Tokyo Museum Collection content rules; © the photographer's estate)
- **Link:** https://museumcollection.tokyo/works/94043/
- **Other links:** https://artplatform.go.jp/collections/W932449, https://ndlsearch.ndl.go.jp/books/R100000002-I000010031555
- **Proof that it exists:** 九龍城の阿片窟 / A Kowloon opium den — 長野 重一 NAGANO Shigeichi — 1958 — ゼラチン・シルバー・プリント (D.O.P) — 253×383mm — 東京都写真美術館 — 10101612 — series 香港
- **What it gives (finder's note, not checked):** A 1958 photo of an opium den in 九龍城 (Kowloon City), from the photographer's Hong Kong series. It is a rare early picture of the drug trade that the City was known for.
- **Notes:** The title says 九龍城, which can mean the Kowloon City district and not only the Walled City. That the den was in the Walled City is INFERRED from the subject; check the image. His book 『香港追憶 : 1958』 (2009) can hold the same series (NDL record in alt_urls). Same museum also holds his '九龍半島の漁村' (1958, W932448), not the City.
