- **What it gives (finder's note, not checked):** The mission's work in the Walled City in 1986: a congregation (menighet), a day-care centre (daghjem) and a reading centre (lesesenter). Also the Hong Kong mission's accounts for 1 Jul 1985 - 30 Jun 1986 (offerings HK$395,366.72; four kindergartens HK$96,500), not split by place.
