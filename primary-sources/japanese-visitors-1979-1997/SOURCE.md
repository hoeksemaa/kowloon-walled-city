### ja-2: 消えた九龍城砦、そして返還

- **Creator:** 阿佐部伸一 (text and photos), asabe.jp, section 「台湾、香港、イギリス、日本」
- **Date:** c.1989-1997 (visits); web text ©2022
- **Kind:** testimony
- **Class:** retrospective (A Japanese reporter tells his own visits and interviews with residents inside the City (c.1989 to the demolition of 23 Mar 1993), with his own photos; the web text has no date of first publication.)
- **Value:** high
- **Language:** ja
- **Access:** open
- **Licence:** © 2022 阿佐部伸一
- **Link:** https://asabe.jp/asiareport/%E6%B6%88%E3%81%88%E3%81%9F%E4%B9%9D%E9%BE%8D%E5%9F%8E%E7%A0%A6%E3%80%81%E3%81%9D%E3%81%97%E3%81%A6%E8%BF%94%E9%82%84/
- **Proof that it exists:** First sentence: 「イギリス、中国、香港政庁の拮抗が生んだアナーキな空間、九龍城砦。」
- **What it gives (finder's note, not checked):** Named residents with their age at the time: the dentist 鄭凌生 (55) of 鄭凌生牙科, who treated at half price, mostly patients from outside; the shopkeeper 陳耀南 (39) on opium (「ここでは『福寿膏』や」); 尹達成 (42) and his secret factory of watch cases and bands for a Japanese buyer; 陳美珊 (21), robbed in a lane in 2nd grade, who says tap water was only for showers and laundry and she carried well water up from the ground floor. Also cables and pipes that drip over the lanes, two cannons of about 3 m in a dark lane, the Yu Lan festival of 11 Aug 1991 and the demolition ceremony of 23 Mar 1993.
- **Notes:** 10 images; captions include 「通路に転がしてあった”文化財”」, 「インチキ時計工場。尹さんは完成品を見たことがない」, 「道路の真ん中に座り込んで抵抗する住民」 and 「「鄭凌生牙科」があったあたりに立つ鄭先生」. The year 1989 is tied to the interview of 陳美珊 (「八十九年」); other visit dates are not exact. Check whether the text first appeared in a magazine. The page names people only in Chinese characters: no romanization given.
- **Same item in the finder files:** en-testimony-1

### ja-6: 超リアル再現映画のメガヒットで再注目　香港の有名スラム街「九龍城砦」はどんな場所だったのか

- **Creator:** Shinchosha (デイリー新潮); testimony of 'Aさん', a Hong Kong man in his 60s
- **Date:** 2024-05-26
- **Kind:** testimony
- **Class:** retrospective (A Hong Kong man who grew up next to the City and went in often from the early 1970s to the clearance tells what he saw.)
- **Value:** medium
- **Language:** ja
- **Access:** open
- **Licence:** © Shinchosha
- **Link:** https://www.dailyshincho.jp/article/2024/05261048/?all=1
- **Other links:** https://www.dailyshincho.jp/article/2024/05261048/?all=1&page=2
- **Proof that it exists:** Page 1 first sentence: 「90年代初頭、香港の空港がまだ町中にあった頃、着陸する飛行機から異様な一角が見えた。」
- **What it gives (finder's note, not checked):** His school friend's flat: 「友人が住んでいたのは2階にある小さな部屋で」, so dark that 「朝でも電気をつける必要があるほど真っ暗でした。」, its windows on a neighbour's wall. Empty syringes on the lane; he took empty bottles to a factory inside to buy soy sauce (「中の工場に空き容器を持参して醤油を買ったこともあります」); a model shop; dark slippery lanes, a chemical smell, coloured water from unlicensed factories, rats; 「中は10分くらいで通り抜けられます。」
- **Notes:** The witness is anonymous. The testimony is on page 2; page 1 is the reporter's history (secondary).

### top-ja-5: 回想　九龍城砦　恐怖の体験

- **Creator:** zhuangyuan (blog 中華　状元への道, exblog)
- **Date:** 2006-08-26
- **Kind:** testimony
- **Class:** retrospective (A visitor's own memory, written in 2006, of a visit to the City in 1991.)
- **Value:** low
- **Language:** ja
- **Access:** open
- **Licence:** © the blogger
- **Link:** https://zhuangyuan.exblog.jp/3648298/
- **Proof that it exists:** 回想　九龍城砦　恐怖の体験
- **What it gives (finder's note, not checked):** A university student and a local friend went in, in 1991, with no wallet or camera. They walked about 50 m into dark alleys, turned back, then climbed a narrow, steep, tight-turning stair to about the third floor. The author saw many dentists (「歯医者がやたら多かった」) and stepped in faeces on the stair (「うんこ踏んじゃった」).
- **Notes:** Retrospective (2006). No photos of the author: the only image is a Wikipedia photo labelled 1989. No month and no entrance given. Found with a WebSearch limited to Japanese blog hosts.
