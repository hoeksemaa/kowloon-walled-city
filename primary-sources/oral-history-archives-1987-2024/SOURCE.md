### usgov-15: Oral history interview with Donald Michael Bishop (Foreign Affairs Oral History Project)

- **Creator:** Donald Michael Bishop, interviewed by Charles Stuart Kennedy; Association for Diplomatic Studies and Training
- **Date:** 2010-09-14
- **Kind:** testimony
- **Class:** retrospective (A later US diplomat tells of his own walk in the City on R&R from Vietnam, about 1969.)
- **Value:** medium
- **Language:** en
- **Access:** open
- **Licence:** (c) 2014 ADST
- **Link:** https://adst.org/OH%20TOCs/Bishop-Donald-Michael1.pdf
- **Proof that it exists:** The Association for Diplomatic Studies and Training, Foreign Affairs Oral History Project, DONALD MICHAEL BISHOP, Interviewed by: Charles Stewart Kennedy, Initial interview date: September 14, 2010
- **What it gives (finder's note, not checked):** pp. 64-66: Maryknoll priest Father Tom Danaher took him into the City; 'streets' no wider than his outstretched arms; the sky hidden by a thicket of illegal electric wires; old men barbecuing pork kabobs to sell to restaurants; unlicensed doctors and dentists, sweat shops, addicts.
- **Notes:** Retrospective (2010) about about 1969 (his R&R from Phu Cat Air Base; the next R&R was at New Year 1970). WebFetch cannot read the PDF text; I read it with pdftotext from the file that WebFetch saved. He was later Assistant Information Officer in Hong Kong, 1981-1983.

- **Kind:** testimony
- **Class:** retrospective (A US consular political officer (Hong Kong 1974-78 and 1982-84) tells later a story that a friend told him.)
- **Value:** low
- **Language:** en
- **Access:** open

- **Link:** https://adst.org/OH%20TOCs/Harter,%20Dennis%20G.toc.pdf
