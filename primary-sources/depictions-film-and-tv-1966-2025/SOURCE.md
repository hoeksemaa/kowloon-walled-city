# Depictions: films and TV shot in or set in the City (primary parts)

The primary twin of [`../../secondary-sources/depictions-film-and-tv-1966-2025/SOURCE.md`](../../secondary-sources/depictions-film-and-tv-1966-2025/SOURCE.md): the records of the
time that its later items quote or show (old letters, minutes and reports, frames filmed at the City),
and the words of witnesses. The items, their secondary facts and their files stay in the secondary folder.

- **Status:** made by the reading of the source search of 2026-11. A checker checked a sample of the
  facts of each chunk.
- **Stored in git:** this file only. The files are under the items in the secondary folder.

## Why this is primary

Each fact is a record of the time or a witness's own words. Testimony given later says its year
("retrospective"). A frame of a film is a record of the place on the day of the shoot; what the frame
shows is the reader's reading of it.
