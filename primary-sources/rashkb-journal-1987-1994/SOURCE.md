# Royal Asiatic Society Hong Kong Branch: President's Reports, 1988-1990

The annual reports of the President of the Royal Asiatic Society Hong Kong Branch, printed in its
Journal (vols. 28 and 29) and open as page images in HKU Hong Kong Journals Online. They record the
Society's tours of the City in 1989, organised by James Hayes. Found by the leads of the reading of
2026-11 (lead L0773).
