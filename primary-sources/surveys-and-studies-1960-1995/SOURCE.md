Field surveys, theses and studies of the City made while it stood.

- **Status:** catalogued on 2026-11-17 by the source search of 2026-11. Nobody has read these items for the repo yet.
  "What it gives" is the short note of the agent that found the item. It is not a checked fact.

  page and a short exact quote.

- **Items:** 4, of which 1 are open now. Value of the set: high.

### ja-1: 香港九龍城スラムの空間構成 (Spatial Structure of the Kowloon Walled City Slum, Hong Kong)

- **Creator:** Shunichi J. Watanabe (渡辺俊一), Masahiro Ibayashi (居林昌宏), Science University of Tokyo (東京理科大学), Department of Architecture
- **Date:** 1993-11
- **Kind:** article
- **Class:** primary (A 12-person team measured the City on site from 22 Jul to 7 Aug 1992, after the residents left, and published the results in 1993.)
- **Value:** high
- **Language:** ja
- **Access:** open
- **Licence:** © City Planning Institute of Japan (日本都市計画学会); free to read on J-STAGE
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.11361/journalcpij.28.439
- **Other links:** https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/journalcpij/28/0/28_439/_pdf, https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/journalcpij/28/0/28_439/_article/-char/ja, https://ndlsearch.ndl.go.jp/books/R000000004-I3862578, https://cir.nii.ac.jp/crid/1390001277344840320
- **Proof that it exists:** Page 439 heading: "74. 香港九龍城スラムの空間構成 / Spatial Structure of the Kowloon Walled City Slum, Hong Kong". Abstract: "The Kowloon Walled City, a Hong Kong's once most notorious slum now waiting for demolition, used to be characterized by high density living (18,000 persons per hectare), high rise structures (14 floors without elevators)..."
- **What it gives (finder's note, not checked):** Field survey of a 2,600 m2 block round 大井街 (about 10% of the City): 19 buildings, 676 units, mean 13.3 storeys, 35.6 units per building, a corridor network of 174 m at the level of the main street (the 4th floor), a table of each building (聯興楼一期 to 八期, X楼, 三興楼, 萬安楼, 萬成楼, 義学大楼 and others) with storeys, units, stair types and plan types. City-wide figures: the survey of 14 Jan 1987 (10,668 households, 31,408 persons, about 70% from eastern Guangdong); the clearance ended 2 Jul 1992 (8,494 units, 9,420 households, 28,198 persons; 51% to public rental housing, 38% bought flats, 11% other); 8,494 units = 6,999 residential (82%), 1,000 non-residential or mixed (11%), 495 other (6%); only one building had a lift.
- **Notes:** 都市計画論文集 no. 28 (1993), pp. 439-444. The PDF is a 6-page image scan with no text layer: read it as images. p.439: "現地調査は、住民立退き後の昨年7月22日から8月7日にかけて、香港政府の許可を得て行われた". Note 4: team of 12 from the authors' lab, with help from Stephen Man-Bun Tang (HK Government), Prof. Anthony Gar-On Yeh (HKU) and Muk-Wang Chow; measured drawings are in 居林昌宏・梅原祐介・中村晋太郎, 東京理科大学 graduation thesis 1992 (see ja-14 for 中村晋太郎). p.440-441: main streets (大路) 5 N-S and 1 E-W, 1-2 m wide, lined with shops and factories; lanes (路地) mostly under 1 m; cables and water pipes hang in bundles over the lanes; buildings 300 or 500, number not known; gaps of about 10 cm with nets for rubbish; NW side Tung Tau Tsuen Road (東頭村道, 22 m), NE side 東正道 (18 m); up to 17 m of slope. p.443: rules among landowners (each side can build over half the lane; no use of another building's air space; height held down by Kai Tak (啓德)); sales and rents by deeds certified by the 街坊福利会. p.444: from the 10th floor of 聯興楼六期, the road route is 135 m + 10 floors of stairs, the corridor route 90 m + 5 floors. Its references are leads: 池谷直人 1992 (ja-15); Frances Bartlett, 'Bringing down a Legend', Asian Property (Mar 1991) pp.30-33; 'Hong Kong's Lost City', Asia Magazine 30:G-19 (3-5 Jul 1992) pp.14-15; Barbara Basler, NYT 16 Jun 1992; 蕭国健『九龍城史論』(1987); the 1982/83 District Board report (ja-18); the Housing Authority paper of Dec 1987 (ja-17). The survey street 大井街 is the street that the repo writes as Tai Chang St (大井街).
