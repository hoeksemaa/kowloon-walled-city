# Gwulo: Andrew Suddaby's four photos of the city, 1 Nov 1987

Four street photos by a visitor, at peak density: two of the north face on
Tung Tau Tsuen Road, and two of the south side across open ground.

- **Link:** https://gwulo.com/photos/by-gallery?gallery=8687&page=1 (gallery
  "1987 Andrew Suddaby's photos"; the four KWC photos are media 22835–22838).
- **Photographer:** Andrew Suddaby. He uploaded the photos to Gwulo on 14 Oct 2015.
- **Date taken:** 1 Nov 1987 (as given on Gwulo).
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-29. I checked all gallery pages (0–2). These four
  are the only KWC photos in it.
- **Copyright:** the pages give no license, so the photographer keeps the
  rights. The comments belong to the people who wrote them.
- **Stored in git:** no. The files are in `local/`, which git ignores.

## Why this is primary

The photographer took the photos at the city and wrote the captions from his
own visit. Gwulo is only the host. The comments are later, and are primary
only where a writer describes their own time in the city.

## Captions (Suddaby's own words)

- 22835: "I took this and the next photograph from the Tung Tau Tsuen Road.
  Several of the small shops on the right were operated by dentists."
- 22836: "In 1987, I had a very quick look round part of the street level area
  inside the Walled City. It didn't last long as I hadn't the faintest idea
  where I was! The entrance was through a narrow opening probably somewhere
  close to the centre of the photograph."
- 22837: "This was the southern side of the Walled City, somewhere close to
  where there were quite a few fortune tellers' stalls. This ground and the
  area where the Walled City stood is now the Kowloon Fortress park."
- 22838: "I think that the hill in the background might be Middle Hill, or
  possibly it's Diamond Hill. The fortune tellers had their stalls very close
  to this spot." Gwulo also tags this photo with Tung Tau Estate.
