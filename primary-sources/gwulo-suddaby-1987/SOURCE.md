# Gwulo: Andrew Suddaby's photos from Tung Tau Tsuen Road, 1 Nov 1987

Two street photos of the north face of the city, by a visitor, at peak density.

- **Link:** https://gwulo.com/media/22835 (the second photo is https://gwulo.com/media/22836)
- **Photographer:** Andrew Suddaby. He uploaded the photos to Gwulo on 14 Oct 2015.
- **Date taken:** 1 Nov 1987 (as given on Gwulo).
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-29.
- **Copyright:** the page gives no license, so the photographer keeps the rights.
  The comments belong to the people who wrote them.
- **Stored in git:** no. The files are in `local/`, which git ignores.

## Why this is primary

The photographer took the photos at the city and wrote the captions from his
own visit. Gwulo is only the host. The comments are later, and are primary
only where a writer describes their own time in the city.

## Captions (Suddaby's own words)

- 22835: "I took this and the next photograph from the Tung Tau Tsuen Road.
  Several of the small shops on the right were operated by dentists."
- 22836: "In 1987, I had a very quick look round part of the street level area
  inside the Walled City. It didn't last long as I hadn't the faintest idea
  where I was! The entrance was through a narrow opening probably somewhere
  close to the centre of the photograph."
